- (decision) RESOLVES CRITICAL 1 — the relevance model is HYBRID with an LLM judgement at its centre: an LLM assesses each piece's relevance, informed by a case theory when the lawyer has written one and by intrinsic signals otherwise. Relevance is a relation to a question, not a property of a document. FR-1's 'no further configuration screen' must be relaxed to admit an OPTIONAL case theory.
- (decision) CONSEQUENCE A (cost/time): an LLM call per document at 100k documents is the dominant cost and latency of the product. Requires a CASCADE — cheap deterministic filters and dedup first, LLM judgement only on the uncertain middle band — plus batching and a cheap model tier. This becomes a cross-cutting NFR, not an implementation detail.
- (decision) CONSEQUENCE B (reproducibility): an LLM judgement is not reproducible by default, but the audit record and the confidence bound both require classifications to be stable and explainable. Therefore every classification must record the RANKING VERSION — model identity, prompt version, temperature, and the returned justification — and re-running a fixed ranking version over a fixed corpus must reproduce the same order. This also defines the 'ranking version' noun the rubric flagged as undefined.
- (decision) CONSEQUENCE C (confidence): self-reported LLM confidence is unreliable and must NOT be the source of the per-document confidence shown to the user. Derive it from score margin or from agreement across judgements. Otherwise the confidence bound rests on a number the model made up.
- (insight) CONSEQUENCE D (sovereignty, the serious one): LLM-judged triage sends EVERY client document to the model. Zero-retention becomes a contract clause doing enormous work, and the Cloud Act exposure stops being about prompts and becomes about the entire corpus. This promotes the fully local model from 'premium tier' (COULD) to arguably necessary for the triage pass — and it is the strongest argument yet for the on-prem thesis, since a hosted competitor has exactly the same problem and cannot solve it.
- (override) CRITICAL, COACH ERROR ORIGINATING IN THE BRAINSTORMING SESSION: the confidence-bound sentence is mathematically FALSE as written. '200 sampled of 1400 discarded, 0 relevant -> risk of having missed a relevant document below 1.5%' misuses the rule of three, which bounds PREVALENCE, not the probability that zero relevant documents remain. At 0/200 the 95% upper bound is 3/200 = 1.5% OF THE POPULATION — i.e. up to ~21 relevant pieces may still be in the discarded set. Being 95% confident that ZERO remain would require sampling ~1330 of 1400. The error propagated from the session memlog into brainstorm-intent.md, brief.md and prd.md, and it is the product's north-star claim.
- (decision) CORRECTED CLAIM: '200 pieces sampled at random from the 1400 discarded; none relevant. With 95% confidence, at most 1.5% of the discarded set — about 21 pieces — is relevant.' Still honest, still valuable, and still more than any competitor offers. Note also that a hypergeometric bound (200/1400 is a large sampling fraction) is tighter than the binomial rule of three and should be the estimator; the finite-population correction is real gain, not pedantry. SM-1 must assert SOUNDNESS, not merely reproducibility.

- (decision) OQ-4 RESOLVED IN APPROACH, no longer blocking. The framing 'needs a statistician rather than a guess' overstated it: a hypergeometric upper confidence bound is standard documented statistics, and the five hard inputs (near-duplicate thread families, census-vs-sample crossover, repeated sampling, population freezing, projection at an unsampled position) are design decisions to be answered explicitly and recorded. METHOD: build it, then VALIDATE BY SIMULATION against populations whose truth is known by construction — a stated C% bound must hold in at least C% of runs, asserted in CI. An estimator that fails does not ship; the counts-only fallback stands. Caveat written into the PRD: simulation validates the estimator against its assumed model, NOT the assumption that a real discarded set resembles the simulated ones — that is what the gold set and calibration (SM-17) are for, and where the honest residual uncertainty lives.
