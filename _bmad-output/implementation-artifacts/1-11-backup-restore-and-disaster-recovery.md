# Story 1.11: Backup, restore and disaster recovery

Status: review

## Story

As a firm running APX on one machine with no ops staff,
I want scheduled encrypted backups, a restore that is exercised not assumed, and a stated storage footprint,
So that a disk failure does not destroy a record I may need in front of a *bâtonnier*.

**Scope in one line:** make the one destructive blessed operation — a restore (AD-46) — unable to silently truncate the evidential record: a **head journal held outside the restorable store** (`APX_HEAD_JOURNAL`) records every *tenant* chain head as it advances; **start-up fails closed** on a missing/unwritable journal (the AD-31 gate) and **reconciles** the live head against it — a live head **behind** the journal is a **truncation**, detected, named, carried onto every export, cleared only by a recorded *override*, never repaired (AD-35). Ship a **complete, tenant-boundary, encrypted logical backup + restore** exercised in CI — restore into an empty store reproduces a *tenant* whose inventory (*denominator*), *audit sequence* and configuration are **identical**, with the AD-43 chain **re-verified** and the head **reconciled** on restore (AD-32). Compute and **state the storage footprint** at the *design target* and **refuse an import that cannot fit** (a pre-flight capacity check). Record backups and surface **"no successful backup within the interval"** as a status. **Not** the `pg_dump`/`upgrade.sh` shell wrappers (deploy) or the *worklist*/home-screen UI (front-end, AD-29); **not** originals/vector-index backup (originals aren't stored yet; the index is 2.8).

> This entire FR is an inference — backup and restore appear in no source document, and their absence is the single most likely way an installation ends a client relationship. The subtlest failure is the one AD-35 names: a dump restore is the one blessed operation that can hard-delete the record, and a truncation to an earlier *consistent* point is **undetectable from inside the database** — every chain link still verifies. The head journal, held where the dump cannot reach, is what makes currency (not just internal consistency) provable.

## Acceptance Criteria

> **Given** a *tenant* at scale, **When** a backup runs, **Then** it produces a complete restorable backup — originals, extracted text, index, *audit record*, *failure register*, configuration — encrypted, inside the *tenant* boundary, on a schedule and on demand (FR-52).
> **And** a restore into an empty installation reproduces a *tenant* whose *denominator*, ranked orders, *audit record* sequence and *confidence bounds* are identical to the source, asserted by CI test at reduced scale (FR-52).
> **And** the storage footprint at the *design target* is computed and stated by the product, and a pre-flight capacity check refuses an *import job* that cannot fit rather than discovering it at 70%.
> **And** backup success or failure is a *worklist* line in the lawyer's language, and a *tenant* with no successful backup within the configured interval says so persistently on the home screen.
> **And** *(failure path)* on a full disk, writes to the append-only stores fail closed, the *import job* halts with a *worklist* line, and no partial state is presented as complete (ties FR-53).

1. **AC1 — The head journal outside the restorable store (AD-35).** Every *tenant* chain head — scope, sequence, chain value, wall-clock timestamp, application & schema versions — is appended to a **head journal** on a volume the dump does not cover (`APX_HEAD_JOURNAL`), and copied onto every backup. A **missing or unwritable** head journal **fails start-up** on the same gate as the encryption key (AD-31/AD-35). On start-up and on restore, the live head is **reconciled** against the journal's latest: a live head **behind** the journal is a **truncation** — surfaced as a status, **named on the face of every export**, cleared only by a recorded *override* with a reason, **never repaired**.
2. **AC2 — Complete, encrypted, tenant-boundary backup + an exercised restore (AD-32).** A logical backup captures a *tenant*'s complete restorable state — *pièces* (provenance + extracted text), the *failure register*, the *audit record*, configuration, scopes, users — inside the *tenant* boundary; content-bearing columns stay **encrypted** in the backup (they are ciphertext at rest). A restore into an **empty** store reproduces the *tenant*: its inventory (the *denominator*), its *audit-record sequence*, and its configuration are **identical**; the AD-43 chain **re-verifies**; and the head is **reconciled** (a clean restore is not a truncation). Asserted by test at reduced scale.
3. **AC3 — Storage footprint stated; pre-flight capacity refusal (AD-32).** The product **computes and states** a *tenant*'s storage footprint at the *design target* (100 000 *pièces*), and a **pre-flight capacity check** refuses an *import job* projected not to fit the available space — rather than discovering it at 70 %.
4. **AC4 — Backup status; overdue is visible (AD-32).** Each backup run is recorded (outcome + timestamp); a *tenant* with **no successful backup within the configured interval** (`backup_interval_hours`, configuration-as-data) reports itself overdue via an admin status surface. (The *worklist*/home-screen rendering is the front-end, AD-29.)
5. **AC5 — Full-disk fail-closed (ties FR-53).** A write to the append-only *audit record* that cannot complete (a full disk) **fails closed** — the transaction rolls back, no partial state is committed, and the condition is surfaced rather than presented as complete. The head-journal append is **after commit** (so a full disk fails the DB write first, closed), and a journal write that fails is surfaced as degraded, never silent.
6. **AC6 — Green and honest.** All existing tests pass; `ruff` + `python -m apx.checks` + `pytest` + fitness green; README documents backup/restore/DR and the head journal; no `pg_dump`/`upgrade.sh` shell (deploy), *worklist*/home-screen UI (AD-29), or originals/index backup (not stored / 2.8) over-build.

## Dev Notes

- **The head journal is the whole point (AD-35).** A dump restore is the one blessed operation that can hard-delete the evidential record, and a truncation to an earlier *consistent* point is **undetectable from inside the database** — the chain still verifies end to end (AD-43 finds a hole in the middle, not a record that now ends earlier). The journal, held where the dump cannot reach and copied onto every backup, records where the head **was**; reconciling the live head against it makes currency provable. A live head **behind** the journal is a truncation: named, carried onto exports, cleared only by an audited *override*, **never repaired**. [Source: ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md#AD-35]
- **Journal after commit, not inside the transaction.** A file append is not transactional with Postgres, so journaling the head happens **after** the audit commits: on a full disk the DB write fails first (fail closed, AC5), so the journal append only runs when the disk had room; a journal append that still fails is surfaced as **degraded** (a gap in the journal weakens future detection but never corrupts state), never silent. This is safer than an in-transaction file write, which would leave the journal ahead of a rolled-back commit and false-positive a truncation. The hook fires on every audited write, so the journal tracks the head **as it advances** (AD-35's "on every append"). [Source: ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md#AD-35, #AD-32 failure path]
- **Backup is logical and per-tenant (AD-32 "inside the tenant boundary").** AD-5's single Postgres makes a *tenant*'s state one consistent snapshot. The backup serializes all of a *tenant*'s rows — content-bearing columns are already **ciphertext** at rest (AD-31), so the backup is encrypted without re-encrypting; restore needs the same key. The **physical** `pg_dump`/`upgrade.sh` wrappers (the production restore path, AD-46) are deploy artifacts; 1.11 builds and **tests** the logical mechanism (round-trip identity, chain re-verify, head reconcile) that the deploy wrapper mirrors. Originals and the vector index are not in the backup because originals are not stored yet and the index is story 2.8 — named so a reviewer does not expect them. [Source: ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md#AD-32, #AD-5, #AD-46; #AD-31]
- **Restore is exercised, not assumed (AD-32).** The CI-scale test restores into an empty store and asserts the *tenant*'s **inventory (the *denominator*)**, **audit sequence** and **configuration** are identical, the chain **re-verifies**, and the head **reconciles** (a clean restore is not a truncation). Ranked orders and *confidence bounds* are named in AD-32 but do not exist yet (epics 4–5); the assertion covers what exists and grows with the pipeline. Collation (AD-5) is asserted at restore where a real `pg_restore` runs (deploy); the logical restore preserves byte-for-byte values. [Source: ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md#AD-32]
- **Capacity is stated, not discovered at 70 % (AD-32).** `footprint(n)` states a per-*pièce* byte estimate × count (a declared constant, honest about being an estimate); the pre-flight `fits(free, projected)` refuses an *import job* whose projected footprint exceeds free space, at submission, not mid-run. The estimate's *value* is a declared figure to be calibrated against the 2.13 timed run; its **presence and the refusal** are the 1.11 deliverable. [Source: ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md#AD-32; epics 2.13]
- **Overdue backup is a status; the rendering is deferred.** `backup_interval_hours` is configuration-as-data (AD-24); the store records each backup outcome and answers "overdue?"; an admin endpoint surfaces it. The *worklist* line and the persistent home-screen warning are the front-end (AD-29); the backend status they read is the 1.11 deliverable. [Source: ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md#AD-32, #AD-24, #AD-29]
- **The AD-49 monotonic counter is not built yet.** The head entry records the wall-clock timestamp and the chain identity; the monotonic per-record counter (AD-49) is a later structural story and joins the head entry when it exists. [Source: ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md#AD-49, #AD-35]
- **What this story does NOT do.** The `pg_dump`/`pg_restore`/`upgrade.sh` shell wrappers + the cron schedule (deploy); the *worklist*/home-screen UI (front-end, AD-29); originals + vector-index backup (not stored / story 2.8); `upgrade.sh`'s pre-migration dump + rollback (AD-46, deploy). Named so the deferral is a decision. [Source: ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md#AD-46, #AD-29; epics 2.8]

### Completion Notes

- **The head journal is the centrepiece, and its mandated test passes.** `HeadJournal` (pure core) records each tenant chain head to an append-only file at `APX_HEAD_JOURNAL`; `reconcile(scope, live_seq)` flags a live head **behind** the journal as a truncation. The store journals heads **after commit** via a SQLAlchemy `before_flush`-capture + `after_commit`-write hook — so a full disk fails the DB write first (closed), and a journal write that fails is surfaced as `journal_degraded`, never silent. The AD-35 mandated test is green: seed → back up early → advance 3 entries (journal at the late head) → simulate a dump restore to the early snapshot → **truncation detected** (`live_seq < journal_seq`), named (`truncation_status.active`), cleared only by an audited override, never re-flagged after acknowledgement.
- **Start-up fails closed on the journal** (`startup_gate`, the AD-31 gate) — a missing or unwritable `APX_HEAD_JOURNAL` refuses the boot; the lifespan reconciles then advances the journal at boot.
- **Backup is logical, per-tenant, ciphertext-preserving.** `backup_tenant` reads rows RAW (so `EncryptedText` columns stay ciphertext — encrypted at rest, verified by a test asserting `apxenc:` prefix); `restore_tenant` inserts RAW into an empty store (refuses a non-empty tenant) and reconciles the head. The round-trip test asserts inventory (denominator), audit sequence and configuration are identical and the chain re-verifies.
- **Capacity is stated and enforced.** `capacity.footprint`/`fits` state the design-target footprint and refuse (HTTP 507) an ingest projected not to fit, with a safety margin — tested at the unit and endpoint level (a mocked tiny free disk). `backup_interval_hours` (config-as-data) + `record_backup`/`backup_status` make "overdue" answerable; `GET /api/admin/dr` surfaces backup-overdue + truncation + footprint (admin-only).
- **Test isolation:** the head journal is deliberately outside the DB, so a session-shared journal would leak heads across tests and false-positive a truncation on a later fresh-DB tenant — the conftest now gives each test its own `APX_HEAD_JOURNAL`.
- **Deferred (documented):** the physical `pg_dump`/`pg_restore`/`upgrade.sh` wrappers + cron (deploy, AD-46); the worklist/home-screen UI (front-end, AD-29); originals + vector-index backup (not stored / story 2.8); the AD-49 monotonic counter (joins the head entry when it exists). AD-32's ranked-orders/confidence-bounds restore assertions cover what exists (epics 4–5 artefacts do not yet).
