- (insight by coach) CORRECTION 2026-07-21 to the entry above ('200 checked, 0 relevant, risk of having missed a relevant document < 1.5%'): that claim is FALSE. Use a hypergeometric estimator, not the binomial rule of three: at this sampling fraction the finite-population correction is a real gain. Left in place rather than rewritten, because this log is append-only and the mistake is instructive — it is exactly the failure mode the product exists to prevent.
